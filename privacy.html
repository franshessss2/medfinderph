Privacy Notice

Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012)

Data we collect

Name, email address, phone number, your reservations (medicine, pharmacy, quantity, notes, contact details) and the payment proof images you upload.

Why we collect it

To create your account, process and track your medicine reservations, notify you of status changes, and let the partner pharmacy verify your payment proof.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in Supabase. Access is restricted by row-level security: you can read your own profile, reservations and notifications; pharmacy administrators can read reservations and payment proofs needed to fulfil them. Payment proofs are kept in a private storage bucket and are only opened through short-lived signed links.

How long we keep it

Until you request deletion of your account.

Your rights

You may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, and deletion of your account and associated records.

Consent record

Consent is given at sign-up and the time it was given is stored with your profile.

Prototype notice

MedFinder PH is a reservation aid, not a licensed online pharmacy. Payment and dispensing are completed at the partner pharmacy. Proof of payment upload is a prototype feature.
